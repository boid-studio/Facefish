Facefish Support

Need help with Facefish? Start with the answers below, or get in touch.

Contact

Email apps@boid.se. We aim to reply within a few business days. Please include your device model, iOS version and a short description of the problem.

Requirements

  • iOS 18 or later.
  • An iPhone or iPad with a TrueDepth (Face ID) camera for face tracking.
  • An external display is optional.

Frequently asked questions

The fish doesn't move with my face.

Check that camera access is allowed in Settings > Facefish > Camera. Face tracking needs a TrueDepth camera and does not work in the iOS Simulator or on devices without Face ID. Make sure your face is well lit and not covered.

The bubbles don't react to sound.

Allow microphone access in Settings > Facefish > Microphone. Bubbles respond to loud low sounds while your mouth is open and to bright high sounds. Audio is analysed live and is never recorded.

The fish is upside down or movement is mirrored the wrong way.

Open Settings (the sliders button). Under General > Phone orientation choose Automatic, Upright or Upside down. Use the Mirror toggle to switch between reflected and direct movement.

How do I use a second device as a remote?

On the main device tap Control > Allow pairing. On the second device turn on Control mode, tap Find main app and choose the main device. Allow Bluetooth access on both devices.

Bluetooth pairing doesn't connect.

Make sure Bluetooth is on and allowed for Facefish on both devices, that both are close together, and that pairing is allowed on the main device. Force-quit and reopen the app on both if needed.
