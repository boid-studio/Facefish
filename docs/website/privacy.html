Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-11]

This policy explains how the Facefish app ("Facefish", "the app") handles information. In short: Facefish does not collect, store, share or sell your personal data, and has no accounts, advertising or analytics.

Camera and face tracking

Facefish uses the TrueDepth camera and Apple's ARKit to follow your head and facial expressions in real time so the fish avatar can mirror them. This processing happens entirely on your device. Camera images and face data are not saved, and are never sent off your device or to us.

Microphone

Facefish measures live microphone levels so that sound can release bubbles from the fish's mouth. Audio is analysed in memory on your device and is never recorded, stored or transmitted.

Bluetooth

Facefish can use Bluetooth to pair with another device running Facefish, so one device can control the avatar on the other. Control commands (such as animations and settings) are sent directly between the two devices over an encrypted connection. They do not pass through any server, and we receive no copy.

Information stored on your device

Facefish saves your app preferences (for example mirror, orientation and calibration settings) locally on your device so they persist between launches. This information stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Information we collect

None. Facefish does not require an account, does not connect to our servers, and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

Facefish does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Permissions

You can review or revoke the camera, microphone and Bluetooth permissions at any time in Settings > Facefish on your device. Without camera access, face tracking will not work.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
